# Negative Mission Statement

## Purpose

This document describes what Scout **intentionally ignores**. It is documentation only.

Scout is not a generic opportunities platform. Scout is not LinkedIn. Scout is not Indeed. Scout is not a women's careers portal.

## What Scout Rejects

Scout intentionally rejects and does not store the following:

### Executive Hiring

- Full-time jobs
- Senior positions (Senior, Staff, Principal, Lead, Architect, Director, Manager, VP, Executive)
- Experienced-hire roles
- 3+ years of experience requirements
- Lateral hires

### Experienced Professionals

- MBA programs
- Career returner programs
- Returnship programs
- Executive education

### Non-Engineering Programs

- Marketing internships
- HR internships
- Finance internships
- Sales internships
- Operations internships
- Consulting internships
- Design internships (non-technical)
- Content internships
- Education internships (non-engineering)
- Policy internships

### Founder Accelerators

- Founder accelerators
- CEO residencies
- Entrepreneur-only programs
- Startup incubators for founders (not interns)

### Generic Events

- Generic conferences
- Generic meetups
- Networking events without actionable application
- Career fairs without student-specific opportunities

### Generic Content

- Career blogs
- News articles
- Generic online courses without active student selection
- MOOCs without application process

### Volunteer Work

- Unpaid volunteer roles
- Non-technical volunteer work
- CSR programs

### Anything Outside Engineering Students

- Opportunities for non-engineering disciplines
- Opportunities for high school students
- Opportunities for PhD/postdoc researchers (unless explicitly student-eligible)
- Opportunities for working professionals with 2+ years experience

## Why

Scout's mission is narrow by design. A narrow mission enables:

- Higher precision extraction
- Better relevance for the target audience
- Clearer product positioning
- Stronger trust with users

**No component may broaden the mission. Only narrow it.**
