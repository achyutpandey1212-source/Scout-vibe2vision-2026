# STARTUP_OPPORTUNITIES_DISCOVERY.md

> **Version:** Scout MVP V2
>
> This document introduces **Startup Opportunities** as a first-class discovery category within Scout.
>
> Rather than treating startup internships as generic internships, Scout intentionally specializes in discovering opportunities from early-stage companies that students rarely find through traditional job platforms.
>
> This category represents one of Scout's strongest competitive advantages.

---

# 1. Vision

Scout should not only help students discover opportunities at Google, Microsoft, Adobe, or Amazon.

It should also uncover opportunities hidden inside India's startup ecosystem.

The goal is to reduce the information gap between students with strong industry networks and students who have the skills but lack access.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Large technology companies have:

- SEO teams
- HR departments
- Career portals
- LinkedIn recruiters
- Marketing budgets

As a result, their internships appear everywhere.

Meanwhile, startups often have:

- Small engineering teams
- Limited hiring budgets
- No dedicated HR
- Poor SEO
- Simple careers pages

Many startups recruit by:

- Founder networks
- Alumni groups
- College societies
- WhatsApp communities
- LinkedIn posts
- Discord servers
- Telegram groups

This creates an information asymmetry.

Excellent students frequently never discover these opportunities.

---

# 3. Scout's Philosophy

Scout is not trying to replace LinkedIn.

Scout is trying to discover opportunities that LinkedIn and Google naturally fail to surface.

Instead of asking:

> Which internship is most famous?

Scout asks:

> Which internship is least likely to be discovered by a talented student?

---

# 4. Objectives

Startup Opportunities should:

- Discover hidden engineering internships
- Surface opportunities from early-stage startups
- Increase discovery diversity
- Reduce dependence on large technology companies
- Improve opportunity uniqueness
- Strengthen Scout's "Hidden Gem" mission

# 7. Search Strategy

Unlike generic internship discovery, Startup Opportunities uses ecosystem-aware searches.

Examples:

```
software engineering internship startup bangalore

frontend internship startup gurgaon

backend internship startup pune

react intern startup bengaluru

nodejs startup internship india

seed funded startup hiring intern

series a startup internship

ai startup internship india

computer vision startup internship

early stage startup careers

engineering intern startup hyderabad

remote startup internship india

startup hiring engineering students

founding engineer intern

startup careers software intern
```

Searches intentionally avoid broad keywords like:

```
software internship india
```

to reduce domination by large companies.

# 10. Discovery Value

Startup opportunities naturally receive higher Discovery Value because:

- Low SEO visibility
- Small hiring teams
- Limited marketing
- Rarely indexed by major job platforms

This aligns perfectly with Scout's mission.

---

# 11. Hidden Gem Integration

Startup Opportunities should positively influence the Hidden Gem Score.

Positive signals include:

- Startup hiring fewer than 100 employees
- Official careers page
- Seed / Series A company
- Remote-friendly engineering internship
- Engineering-first company
- University incubated startup
- Venture-backed startup

Negative signals include:

- Mass hiring portals
- Third-party reposts
- Generic job aggregators

# 14. Success Metrics

The category should be measured separately.

Suggested KPIs:

- Startup opportunities discovered
- Unique startups discovered
- Official startup career pages found
- Average Hidden Gem Score
- Average Student Relevance
- Remote internship percentage
- Venture-backed startups discovered
- Incubator-linked startups discovered

Success is not measured by volume alone.

Success is measured by discovering opportunities students would otherwise miss.
