# PHASE 9 — Content & Voice System

> **Purpose**
>
> Great products are remembered not only for how they look, but also for how they speak.
>
> Scout's voice should feel consistent across every page, button, notification, empty state, loading screen, and interaction.
>
> Users should never feel like different parts of the product were written by different people.
>
> This document defines Scout's communication system.

---

# 1. Brand Voice

Scout communicates like a trusted mentor.

Not like a recruiter.

Not like a corporate website.

Not like an AI assistant.

The product should feel calm, knowledgeable, approachable, and quietly confident.

Users should always feel that Scout is helping them discover opportunities—not trying to sell them something.

---

## Brand Personality

Scout is:

- Calm
- Helpful
- Human
- Honest
- Intelligent
- Curious
- Optimistic
- Encouraging
- Professional
- Confident

Scout is **not**:

- Corporate
- Robotic
- Overly casual
- Funny for the sake of being funny
- Sales-driven
- Loud
- Dramatic
- Overly technical
- Overly enthusiastic

---

# 2. Writing Philosophy

Scout speaks with clarity.

Every sentence should have a purpose.

Writing should reduce friction rather than add personality for its own sake.

Instead of sounding impressive, Scout should sound trustworthy.

The writing should quietly reinforce confidence without exaggeration.

---

## Core Principle

> **Every sentence should reduce uncertainty.**

Whenever a user reads something inside Scout, it should answer one of these questions:

- What is happening?
- Why is this happening?
- What should I do next?
- How does this help me?

If a sentence doesn't achieve one of these goals, it probably shouldn't exist.

---

# 3. Writing Principles

## Human First

Write like a thoughtful person.

Never like documentation.

Example:

❌ AI successfully processed your recommendation profile.

✅ Scout has finished preparing your recommendations.

---

## Explain Benefits

Focus on outcomes.

Not implementation.

Example:

❌ AI-powered recommendation engine.

✅ Recommendations tailored to your profile.

---

## Be Honest

Scout never exaggerates.

Avoid unrealistic promises.

Never claim:

- Best opportunities
- Guaranteed success
- Perfect recommendations
- Revolutionary AI

Instead communicate:

- Relevant opportunities
- Personalized recommendations
- Carefully selected matches

---

## Keep It Short

UI copy should be concise.

Guidelines:

Headings

3–8 words

Supporting text

Maximum 20–25 words

Buttons

1–3 words

Users should scan the interface, not read paragraphs.

---

## Speak With Confidence

Avoid uncertain wording.

Instead of:

You might want to...

Use:

Consider applying.

Instead of:

We think this could help.

Use:

This opportunity aligns with your profile.

---

## Guide, Don't Push

Scout recommends.

Scout never pressures.

Instead of:

Apply Now Before It's Too Late!

Use:

Apply on Official Website

# 5. Grammar & Style

## Use Contractions

Preferred:

- You're
- We'll
- It's
- Don't
- Can't
- We'll

Avoid:

- You are
- We will
- It is
- Do not
- Cannot

Contractions make Scout feel conversational and natural.

---

## Sentence Case

Use sentence case throughout the interface.

Examples:

Today's Brief

Saved Opportunities

Discover Opportunities

Opportunity Details

Avoid:

TODAY'S BRIEF

Today's brief

discover opportunities

---

## Punctuation

Use punctuation intentionally.

Avoid excessive exclamation marks.

Preferred:

Opportunity saved.

Not:

Opportunity saved!!!

---

## Emoji Usage

Emoji should remain minimal.

Approved icons:

⭐ Top Match

💎 Hidden Gem

🚀 Stretch Goal

⚡ Quick Win

🌱 Confidence Builder

Emoji should only serve recognition.

Never decoration.

Never inside buttons.

Never inside paragraphs.

---

# 6. UI Writing Rules

Every interface element follows the same principles.

---

## Buttons

Buttons should describe the action.

Preferred:

Get Started

Continue

Save

View Details

Apply

Discover

Upload Resume

Replace Resume

Avoid:

Click Here

Submit

Proceed

Next Step

---

## Navigation

Navigation labels should remain short and familiar.

Approved labels:

Dashboard

Discover

Saved

Profile

---

## Form Labels

Labels should be descriptive.

Avoid placeholder-only forms.

Example:

Email Address

Password

College

Skills

Career Goals

---

## Confirmation Messages

Keep confirmations calm.

Examples:

✓ Opportunity saved.

✓ Resume uploaded.

✓ Profile updated.

✓ Preferences saved.

Avoid unnecessary celebration.

---

## Loading Messages

Loading should explain progress.

Avoid:

Loading...

Preferred:

Scout is preparing today's recommendations.

Searching trusted sources.

Building your dashboard.

Almost ready.

---

## Error Messages

Never blame the user.

Preferred:

We couldn't upload your resume.

Please try again.

Instead of:

Upload failed.

---

## Empty States

Every empty state should include:

- Clear headline
- Brief explanation
- One primary action

Example:

Nothing saved yet.

Bookmark opportunities while exploring and they'll appear here.

Discover Opportunities →

---

## Success States

Success should reassure.

Not interrupt.

Examples:

Opportunity saved.

Changes saved.

Resume updated.

Recommendation refreshed.

Toast notifications are preferred over modal dialogs.

# 9. Accessibility Through Language

Writing should remain accessible.

Guidelines:

- Plain English.
- Avoid unnecessary jargon.
- Short sentences.
- Clear actions.
- Avoid ambiguous wording.
- Never rely solely on color to communicate meaning.

Every message should remain understandable on its own.

---

# 10. Final Philosophy

Scout's writing should feel invisible.

Users should never stop to admire the copy.

They should simply understand what is happening, why it matters, and what to do next.

Every sentence should quietly reinforce Scout's role as a trusted guide through the world of opportunities.

When users leave Scout, they shouldn't remember clever marketing slogans.

They should remember one feeling:

> **Scout always made the next step obvious.**
